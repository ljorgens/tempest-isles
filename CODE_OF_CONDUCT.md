# Code of Conduct

Tempest Isles is a small hobby project, and everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests or
anywhere else on this repository) is expected to be kind and respectful.

**Not OK:** harassment, insults, personal attacks, discriminatory or sexual language or imagery,
publishing someone's private information, spam, and deliberately derailing discussions.

**What happens:** the maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock threads, close issues or pull
requests, and block anyone who doesn't follow this code, at their discretion.

**Reporting:** email driftwoodgames@proton.me. Reports are kept private.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
